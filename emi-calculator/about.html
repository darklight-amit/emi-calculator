About DarkLight Financial Tools

DarkLight provides straightforward financial calculators and resources to help users make informed borrowing decisions.

Our mission

We want to make loan planning easier by offering free tools that help users estimate monthly payments and understand total repayment costs.

What we offer

  • EMI calculator for loans with fixed interest rates
  • Clear explanations of interest cost and total payment
  • Simple budgeting support for borrowers

Who can benefit

Whether you are considering a home loan, car loan, personal loan, or education loan, our calculator helps you estimate costs before you commit.

All tools are free to use and accessible on both desktop and mobile devices.
