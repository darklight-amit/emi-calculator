Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the fixed amount you pay every month to repay a loan over a specific period.

How is EMI calculated?

The calculator uses the loan amount, annual interest rate, and tenure to compute monthly payments, total interest, and total repayment.

Can I use this for home, car, or personal loans?

Yes. This tool works for any fixed-rate loan where the interest rate stays the same during the loan term.

Do I need to enter any personal data?

No personal data is required. Only loan numbers are entered locally in your browser for instant calculation.
